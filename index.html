The problem

What is worth remembering is read once, and lost.

Most valuable knowledge lives in AI chats, documentation sites, blogs and Wikipedia, scattered across tabs, sessions and tools. Once read, it is buried or hard to find again.

Lexicora lets you:

  • capture web content directly from the browser
  • clean and structure it automatically
  • store it in a fast, searchable local knowledge base
  • retrieve it instantly when you need it

All without changing how you already browse or use AI tools. Lexicora is intentionally opinionated: not a general-purpose note-taking app, not a workspace, not a social platform. It is a tool for turning web content into searchable knowledge with as little friction as possible.

Features

Capture, structure, retrieve.

Everything between reading something and finding it again, in the browser side panel.

  • Two ways to save

    Capture a page and its content lands in an editor you can annotate, so it survives the page changing or disappearing. Or bookmark it, and keep only the title, link, site and description.

  • Save from anywhere

    From the side panel, the toolbar popup, the right-click menu or a keyboard shortcut. The menu and the shortcut can keep just the text you selected. In Chrome and Edge, Lexicora can also suggest capturing a page you have spent a while on.

  • Cleaned, not scraped

    Lexicora keeps the article with its headings, paragraphs, lists, code, tables, callouts and images, and leaves out the menus, sidebars and ads around it.

  • Sort it your way

    Topics group what belongs together. Tags cut across them. Pin what you return to, archive what you are done with, favourite what matters.

  • Find it again

    Search runs over titles, tags, descriptions, sites and dates. Narrow it to one site with site:react.dev. Back on a site, the side panel shows what you already saved from it.

  • Write in it

    Every entry has a rich block editor: headings, lists, tables, code blocks with syntax highlighting and callouts. Drag text and images in from the page you are reading.

  • Take it with you

    Export a note, a topic or the whole library as Markdown with front matter that Obsidian reads as properties. Or back up as JSON and import it again, choosing what wins when something exists in both.

  • Keyboard-driven

    Browser-wide shortcuts open the panel, capture and bookmark. Inside the panel, single keys move around, search and edit. Light, dark or system theme, painted correctly from the first frame.

  • Offline-first

    The library lives in your browser's own database for the extension and works fully offline. Search and retrieval never wait on a network.

How it works

Browse as you always do.

Most interactions happen inside the browser side panel, keeping context and flow intact.

  1. Browse the web AI chats, docs, articles, wikis, anywhere.
  2. Capture it The page, just the text you selected, or a bookmark of it, from the side panel, the toolbar popup, the right-click menu or a keyboard shortcut.
  3. Lexicora keeps the content and its structure Headings, paragraphs, lists, code, tables, callouts and images stay. Menus, sidebars and ads do not.
  4. It lands in a topic Editable and searchable at once.
  5. Come back to it When you are back on a site, the side panel shows what you already saved from it.

Design principles

Opinionated on purpose.

  • Capture-first

    Saving knowledge should be effortless.

  • Retrieval-first

    Finding saved knowledge must be instant.

  • Speed over complexity

    No heavy UI, no deep nesting.

  • Flat structure by design

    Intentional limits improve clarity.

  • Keyboard-driven

    Minimal mouse usage.

  • Browser-native

    Works where knowledge is consumed.

  • Local-first

    The library lives on your device. Version 1 works fully offline and sends nothing anywhere.

  • AI-optional

    Useful without AI. AI features are currently disabled, and nothing in this version uses them.

Keyboard

Browser-wide shortcuts.

These work from any page. Inside the side panel there are more, and all of them are listed in Settings → Keyboard Shortcuts.

ActionmacOSWindows and Linux
Open or close the side panel⌃ ⇧ LAlt Shift L
Capture the selection, or the page⌃ ⇧ SAlt Shift S (Firefox: Alt Shift W)
Bookmark the page⌃ ⇧ DAlt Shift D

Shortcuts can be changed in your browser's own extension shortcut settings.

Privacy

It is yours.

Lexicora keeps everything you capture on your own computer. It has no accounts, no servers and no analytics, and it sends nothing about you or your browsing anywhere.

Read the privacy policy
  • Your library is stored by your browser, on this device.
  • The extension makes no network requests of its own.
  • A page is read only when you capture or bookmark it, and only the page open in front of you, never other tabs and never in the background.
  • Delete everything at once with Settings → Storage → “Clear all data”, or by removing the extension.
  • If a future version ever sends anything anywhere, the policy says so first, and the feature is yours to switch on.

Status

Not the goal

What Lexicora is not

A full project management tool, a generic note-taking app, a deeply nested workspace or a social platform. Those problems are already solved elsewhere, often at the cost of speed and clarity.
