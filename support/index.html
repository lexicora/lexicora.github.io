Support

Something not working, or a question about Lexicora? Here is where to go.

  • Report a problemOpen an issue on GitHub. Saying which browser you use and what you did before it went wrong is usually enough to reproduce it.
  • Write an emailhello@tgmaurer.dev, for questions, for anything private, or if you have no GitHub account.
  • The repositoryThe source, the open issues and what is planned. Lexicora is developed in the open.

Before you write

The answer may already be in the extension: Settings → Help has the getting-started page, the FAQ, and tips and tricks.

If you report a problem, these help: your browser and its version, Lexicora's version (Settings → About), and the steps that led to it. If it is about capturing a page, the page's address helps too, if you are happy to share it.

Finding the side panel

Click Lexicora's button in the toolbar, or press ⌃ ⇧ L on macOS or Alt Shift L on Windows and Linux.

If the button is not in the toolbar, pin it. In Chrome and Edge, open the Extensions button (the puzzle piece) and select the pin next to Lexicora. In Firefox, open the Extensions button, then Lexicora's gear menu, and choose Pin to Toolbar.

Your data

  • Your library exists only on your device. Lexicora has no account and no server, so nothing can be recovered from anywhere else. Export a backup now and then.
  • Settings → Export saves the library as a JSON backup, which Settings → Import reads back, or as Markdown.
  • Settings → Storage → “Clear all data” deletes everything, as does removing the extension. Neither can be undone.

How Lexicora handles your data is in the privacy policy, and what changed in each version is in the changelog.
